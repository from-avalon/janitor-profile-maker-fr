Basic presets

Colour schemes and accents. Each appends a labelled block inside your <style>; toggle one off and its block is removed — anything you wrote around it stays put.

Advanced templates

Complete profile designs by community creators, split into parts. Add the whole template, or open it and take only the pieces you want — each part brings its own markup and CSS.

Only changes the preview, never your code. Set these to match your real profile so you are designing against your own content. Your About Me contents live in the editor on the right, not here.

Use your saved profile

Import a Webpage, Single File (.mhtml) save from your JanitorAI profile. It stays on this device and loads your real profile content into the preview.

MHTML is recommended because it keeps avatars and card images in one file. HTML is accepted, but its saved image folder cannot be read by a browser.
